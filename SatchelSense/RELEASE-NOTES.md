## 1.0.0-rc.5 - Middle-click to hide

- Middle-click any item on the grid to add it to your hidden list without opening settings.
- Works with the grid locked or unlocked, outside combat.
- To bring an item back, open **Items & Keys**, find it near the bottom of the list, and click **Restore**.
- Auto mode closes the gap after hiding an item. Manual mode keeps its saved slot.
- Hidden items stay hidden after reloading or logging back in. Their item keybindings remain assigned.
- Added item and pouch tooltip reminders for the middle-click shortcut and where to restore hidden items.
- Existing settings, saved layouts and keybindings carry over.
